Our Story

Why Master Artist exists

One studio, several ways in — taught by people who still practise the craft they teach.

Master Artist was started by Sabrina Hew, an international recording artist who has recorded and performed on stage in the United States. These days her focus is different: helping children and adults find their own voice — musically, and through art. That's the idea the whole studio is built on.

One studio, several ways in, taught by people who still practise the craft they teach — not just instructors, working artists. Classes stay small and personal, and programmes run in short terms, so your child can try one discipline, switch to another, or take two, without being locked into a long commitment.

It's also where friendships are built. Many of our students grow up together in the studio, meeting week after week, cheering each other on at recitals and exhibitions — the community ends up mattering just as much as the craft.

Meet the Studio

Taught by working creatives

Every class is led by someone who still practises the craft they teach.

Sabrina Hew

Founder — Voice & Art

Founder of Master Artist and an international recording artist, with recordings and concert performances across the United States. These days her passion is helping children and adults find their own voice — musically and in art — and that is a large part of why Master Artist exists.

Cheong Chui Yen

Art

Trained in fine arts, with years of experience teaching drawing and painting — from young children through to adults. Technique first, always taught with patience.

Alden

Photography

Founder of WaffleBox Productions, with years of experience shooting graduations, events, food and products. Alden has an eye for turning everyday moments into images worth keeping — and he's looking to share that knowledge and love for photography with anyone curious enough to pick up a camera.

Zimin

Videography

Behind the camera and in the edit, Zimin brings a filmmaker's eye to every project — turning raw footage into stories worth watching. From concept to final cut, he's passionate about helping students find their own visual voice and tell stories that stick.
