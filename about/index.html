Master Artist Academy

Why Master Artist exists

One studio, several ways in, taught by people who still practise the craft they teach.

Book a RM50 trialMeet the teachers

Master Artist was started by Sabrina Hew, an international recording artist who has recorded and performed on stage in the United States. Her focus now is helping children and adults find their own voice, musically and through art.

Every class is taught by working artists, not just instructors. Classes stay small and personal, so students try a discipline, stay with it and see real progress.

It is also where friendships are built. Many students grow up together in the studio, week after week, and cheer each other on at the showcase.

What makes it different

A studio built around real craft

Taught by working artists

Every class is led by someone who still performs, paints, photographs, films or bakes, not just an instructor.

Small, personal classes

Art classes of eight at most, so every student is seen and you'll actually get better.

Parents welcome to watch

Sit in on a class any time. Nothing happens behind a closed door.

Real rooms, real equipment

A recording studio, a working kitchen, and the room with the view on the second floor.

Try a class for RM50

A showcase every term

Students exhibit and perform what they have made for family and friends.
