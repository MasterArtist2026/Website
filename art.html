01 · Art

Technique first, imagination always

Art class covers drawing, painting, sculpture and mixed media, taught by Cheong Chui Yen, who trained in fine arts and has years of experience teaching the subject — from young children through to adults.

Ages 4–8

Art — Young Kids

Drawing and painting, building confidence with line, shape and colour. Max 8 per class.

Ages 9–15

Art — Older Kids

Technique and portfolio — composition, form and developing their own ideas. Max 8 per class.

What you'll learn

  • Drawing & painting — from first sketches to finished pieces
  • Sculpture & mixed media
  • Line, form, colour and composition — real, transferable skills
  • A portfolio of their own work, for older students

Is this right for your child?

  • Complete beginners
  • Students who already draw or paint and want to sharpen their craft
  • Children who enjoy patient, hands-on guidance
  • Ages 4 to 15 — adults, see Adult Mornings

Classes stay small and hands-on, with plenty of individual guidance — the goal is always patient, careful teaching rather than rushing a student through a finished piece. All materials are included.

02 · Graphic Design

Graphic Design

Digital design fundamentals for ages 10 and up — the creative skill that quietly turns into a career. Students learn to design on screen with the same care for composition and colour they'd bring to a painting.

What you'll learn

  • Digital design fundamentals
  • Layout, typography and colour
  • Working from a real brief
  • Turning an idea into a finished design

Is this right for your child?

  • Ages 10 and up
  • Children who love drawing and want to go digital
  • Anyone curious about how posters, logos and apps are made
