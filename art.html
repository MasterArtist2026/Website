Art Class

Technique first, imagination always

Art class covers drawing, painting, sculpture and mixed media, taught by Cheong Chui Yen, who trained in fine arts and has years of experience teaching the subject — from young children through to adults.

What you'll learn

  • Drawing & painting — from first sketches to finished pieces
  • Sculpture & mixed media
  • Line, form, colour and composition — real, transferable skills
  • Developing their own ideas, once the technique is there

Is this right for your child?

  • Complete beginners
  • Students who already draw or paint and want to sharpen their craft
  • Children who enjoy patient, hands-on guidance
  • Ages 4 through to adult

Classes stay small and hands-on, with plenty of individual guidance — the goal is always patient, careful teaching rather than rushing a student through a finished piece.
