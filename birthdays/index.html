How it works

Tell us the party, the numbers and a date. We check the room and confirm on WhatsApp.

A RM200 deposit holds the date and comes off the total.

Good to know

Can we bring a cake?

Yes. The last 30 minutes are yours in the room for cake and candles.

Do parents stay?

Parents are welcome to stay and watch.

Allergies

Tell us about allergies when you book. Bake parties are plant-based, made in a shared kitchen where gluten and nuts may be present.

Saturdays

We are closed on Saturdays, so parties run on Sundays and weekdays.
