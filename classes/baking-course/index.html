Six weekly sessions in the L1 kitchen, up to 20 bakers. Each week builds on the last, and the final week is a showcase bake you choose yourself.

What a term looks like

6-week cohort; opens when 6 are enrolled.

Weeks 1–2 · Foundations

Doughs, proving and shaping; the basics every plant-based bake depends on.

Weeks 3–4 · Technique

Enriched doughs, laminating and plant-based swaps for eggs and butter.

Weeks 5–6 · Showcase

Plan and bake a showcase piece of your own choice.
