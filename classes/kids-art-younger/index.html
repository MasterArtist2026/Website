What a term looks like

Monthly, with a showcase every 10–12 weeks.

Level 1 · Explore

Line, shape and colour mixing; drawing from objects on the table; paint, pastel and clay.

Level 2 · Build

Observation drawing, simple composition and layering paint; a small series on one theme.

Level 3 · Create

Their own project from sketch to finished piece, shown at the term showcase.
