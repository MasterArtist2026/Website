## Medium description (≈120 words)
Master Artist is a one-stop creative hub in Desa Sri Hartamas, Kuala Lumpur — a
short drive from Mont Kiara, Bangsar and Damansara Heights. Every class is taught
by a working creative: voice with founder and recording artist Sabrina Hew, piano
and instruments in private soundproof rooms (no exams — music taught to be loved),
and the Mont Kiara Orchestra, a weekly Friday ensemble open to musicians of any
school. Art classes for ages 4–15 are capped at 8 students with all materials
included, alongside Graphic Design (10+), Photography and Videography (12+) in
small groups of 6, Adult Mornings, and baking, painting and craft workshops.
Parents are welcome to watch any class. Try any class for RM50, credited in full
to your first month.
