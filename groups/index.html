How a team day works

Pick a package, tell us your numbers and a date, and we plan the day with you on WhatsApp.

For bigger groups we run streams in parallel in the L2 front room, L1 front room, L1 kitchen and L2 recording studio, so everyone can choose what they make.

Add the team dinner package to have dinner at Savor downstairs with an evening workshop.

Good to know

Fridays

Friday team days finish by 12:30pm; the building closes at 5pm on Fridays and all day Saturday.

Minimum numbers

Team days run from 6 people. Each extra person over a package is RM100.

Payment
