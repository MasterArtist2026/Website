What makes learning here different.

Taught by working artists

Every class is led by someone who still performs, paints, photographs, films or bakes — not just an instructor.

Small, personal classes

Private 60-minute music lessons, art classes of 8 at most, media groups of 6 — every child is seen.

Short terms — try, switch or take two

Programmes run in short terms, so your child isn't locked in. Try one discipline, switch, or add another.

Parents welcome to watch

All questions

How much does a trial class cost?

RM50. If you enrol afterward, that RM50 is credited in full to your first month.

What ages do you teach?

From age 4 through to adults. Each programme lists its own age range, so you can see the right starting point at a glance.

Does my child need any experience or their own equipment to start?

No — a trial class is designed for complete beginners, and everything you need for that first class is provided. If you continue with an instrument-based class like piano, we'll let you know what's useful to have at home once you've enrolled.

Can I stay and watch a class?

Yes — parents are welcome to sit in on any class, any time. Nothing happens behind a closed door.
