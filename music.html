The Programmes

Find your voice, or your instrument

Two ways into Music at Master Artist — singing and piano — both taught by people who still perform and play professionally.

01 · Vocal

Vocal Class

Ages 4+

Vocal class is taught directly by Sabrina Hew, the founder of Master Artist — a Christian international singer/songwriter and composer who has recorded and performed on stage in the United States.

Students work on breathing, pitch, tone and stage confidence, building their own voice one session at a time rather than copying someone else's. Lessons are suited to complete beginners as well as students who already sing and want to sharpen their technique.

Because Sabrina is still performing and recording herself, what's taught in class comes from someone actively doing the work, not just teaching the theory of it.

02 · Piano

Piano Class

Ages 4+

Piano lessons are paced to wherever a student is starting from, and structured across three stages:

Beginner

Note-reading, hand position and simple pieces — a first, unintimidating introduction to the instrument.

Intermediate

Building technique, sight-reading and musicality, with more complex repertoire as confidence grows.

Advanced

Refining performance-level pieces, deeper music theory, and expression — for students ready to go further.
