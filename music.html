01 · Voice

Voice with Sabrina Hew

Taught directly by Sabrina Hew, the founder of Master Artist — a Christian international singer/songwriter and composer who has recorded and performed on stage in the United States. Students build their own voice one session at a time, rather than copying someone else's.

What you'll learn

  • Breath support & control — the foundation every singer works from
  • Pitch & ear training — hearing and hitting the right note
  • Tone & vocal placement — a clear, healthy, resonant sound
  • Stage presence — performing with confidence

Is this right for your child?

  • Complete beginners who love to sing
  • Students who already sing and want to sharpen their technique
  • Anyone who wants more confidence performing
  • Ages 5 through to adult

Because Sabrina still performs and records, what's taught in class comes from someone actively doing the work. Prefer to sing with a friend? Group Voice (two or more together) is RM340 / month per person.

02 · Piano & Instruments

Play, create & compose

Piano and instrument lessons are paced to wherever a student is starting from — no exams, just real playing, creating and composing. Lessons follow three stages, so beginners are never overwhelmed and experienced players keep being stretched.

Stage 1

Beginner

Note-reading, hand position and simple pieces — a first, unintimidating introduction to the instrument.

Stage 2

Intermediate

Building technique, sight-reading and musicality, with more complex repertoire as confidence grows.

Stage 3

Advanced

Performance-level pieces, deeper music theory and expression — for students ready to go further.

What you'll learn

  • Reading music and correct hand position
  • Technique and sight-reading
  • Musicality and expression
  • Music theory, at the advanced stage

Is this right for your child?

  • First-time players — no experience needed
  • Students who already play and want structure
  • Players who want to create and compose, not sit exams
  • Ages 5 through to adult

03 · Instruments

Violin & strings

Violin lessons build the fundamentals from the ground up before moving into repertoire, so students develop clean technique rather than habits that need to be undone later. Lessons are paced to the individual student.

What you'll learn

  • Posture and bow hold
  • Tone production and intonation
  • Ear training and finger placement
  • From open-string exercises to first pieces

Is this right for your child?

  • Complete beginners
  • Students with some experience who want clean technique
  • Learners who benefit from individual attention
  • Ages 5 through to adult

04 · Workshop & Jam Session

Mont Kiara Orchestra

A weekly workshop and jam session every Friday at 3pm, where musicians who usually learn on their own come together to play as an orchestra — learning pieces together, listening to each other, and discovering what it feels like to be part of something bigger than a solo lesson. All instruments are welcome, and you don't need to take lessons at Master Artist to join.

What you'll do

  • Play in an ensemble — learn pieces together as one orchestra
  • Listen and keep time with other musicians
  • Jam and have fun with music outside a one-on-one lesson
  • Perform live — 2 to 4 orchestra and ensemble performances a month, including evenings at Savor restaurant

Is this right for your child?

  • Anyone already learning an instrument — any type
  • Musicians from any school or teacher — RM200 / month (RM50 a rehearsal)
  • Players ready to learn pieces alongside others
  • Anyone who wants real performance experience and friends through music

Individual lessons build the skills; the orchestra is where they come alive. Students rehearse together every Friday and perform 2 to 4 times a month for family and friends — including evening shows at Savor restaurant.
