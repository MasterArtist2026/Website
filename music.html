The Programmes

Find your voice, or your instrument

Three ways into Music at Master Artist — singing, piano and violin — taught by people who still perform and play professionally.

01 · Vocal

Vocal Class

Ages 4+

Vocal class is taught directly by Sabrina Hew, the founder of Master Artist — a Christian international singer/songwriter and composer who has recorded and performed on stage in the United States.

Students work on breathing, pitch, tone and stage confidence, building their own voice one session at a time rather than copying someone else's. Lessons are suited to complete beginners as well as students who already sing and want to sharpen their technique.

  • Breath Support & Control — building the foundation every singer works from.
  • Pitch & Ear Training — hearing and hitting the right note, every time.
  • Tone & Vocal Placement — developing a clear, healthy, resonant sound.
  • Stage Presence — performing with confidence in front of an audience.

Because Sabrina is still performing and recording herself, what's taught in class comes from someone actively doing the work, not just teaching the theory of it.

02 · Piano

Piano Class

Ages 4+

Piano lessons are paced to wherever a student is starting from, and structured across three stages:

Beginner

Note-reading, hand position and simple pieces — a first, unintimidating introduction to the instrument.

Intermediate

Building technique, sight-reading and musicality, with more complex repertoire as confidence grows.

Advanced

Refining performance-level pieces, deeper music theory, and expression — for students ready to go further.

03 · Violin

Violin Class

Ages 4+

Violin lessons build the fundamentals from the ground up — posture, bow hold, tone production and intonation — before moving into repertoire, so students develop clean technique rather than habits that need to be undone later.

As with our other Music classes, lessons are paced to the individual student, with complete beginners and those with some prior experience both welcome. Students progress from simple open-string exercises through to their first pieces, building the ear training and finger placement that everything else in violin depends on.

Sessions are one-on-one or in small groups, giving each student the individual attention needed to correct technique early and build confidence on the instrument.
