Mont Kiara Orchestra

A weekly workshop and jam session every Friday at 3pm, where musicians who usually learn on their own come together to play as an orchestra — learning pieces together, listening to each other, and discovering what it feels like to be part of something bigger than a solo lesson. All instruments are welcome, and you don't need to take lessons at Master Artist to join.

What you'll do

  • Play in an ensemble — learn pieces together as one orchestra
  • Listen and keep time with other musicians
  • Jam and have fun with music outside a one-on-one lesson
  • Perform live — 2 to 4 orchestra and ensemble performances a month, including evenings at Savor restaurant

Is this right for your child?

  • Anyone already learning an instrument — any type
  • Musicians from any school or teacher — RM200 / month (RM50 a rehearsal)
  • Players ready to learn pieces alongside others
  • Anyone who wants real performance experience and friends through music

Individual lessons build the skills; the orchestra is where they come alive. Students rehearse together every Friday and perform 2 to 4 times a month for family and friends — including evening shows at Savor restaurant.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll get back to you during opening hours.

Who can join the Mont Kiara Orchestra?

Any musician who is learning an instrument — from any school or teacher, not just Master Artist. All instruments are welcome.

When does the orchestra rehearse?

Every Friday at 3pm at the Master Artist studio in Desa Sri Hartamas, above Savor.

How often does the orchestra perform?

There are 2 to 4 orchestra and ensemble performances a month, including evening performances at Savor restaurant.

How much does it cost?

Ensemble membership is RM200 a month (RM50 per rehearsal). Enrolled Master Artist students pay an add-on rate of RM120 a month (RM30 per rehearsal).

Can we try a rehearsal first?

Yes — message us on WhatsApp and come to a Friday rehearsal to see how it feels before joining.
