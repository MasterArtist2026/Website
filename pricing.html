Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll get back to you during opening hours.

Is the trial fee refunded if we enrol?

Yes — the RM50 trial is credited in full against your first month when you enrol, so trying us costs nothing.

How does monthly billing work?

The monthly fee covers four weekly sessions. Art & Design is sold in 4-session packs, and session packs are valid for 2 months.

Do you offer sibling or multi-class discounts?

15% off a brother or sister, 15% off a second programme, and 10% off when you prepay a full term.

What is the Founding 50?

The first fifty students register free (normally RM100 per family) and get their first month half price — with their rate protected for 12 months.

Is there a deposit or materials fee?

No deposit, and all materials are included in every class.

What if we miss a class?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will help you reschedule. See our Terms & Enrolment for details.
