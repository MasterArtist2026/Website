Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll get back to you during opening hours.

Is the trial fee refunded if we enrol?

Yes — the RM50 trial fee is credited in full to your first month if you enrol afterward.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. Programmes run in short terms, so your child can try one discipline, switch to another, or take two, without a long commitment.

Can siblings join together?

Yes — with five disciplines under one roof, siblings can often be taught in the same hour. Ask us about sibling arrangements.

What if we miss a class?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can. Our make-up class and refund policy is set out in our Terms & Enrolment.
