Privacy and PDPA notice

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Who we are

Master Artist ("we", "us") runs weekly classes (Master Artist Academy) and workshops (Master Artist Studio Sessions) from our studio in Desa Sri Hartamas, Kuala Lumpur. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, and how it is handled, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What we collect

Booking and enquiry details you give us: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, the class or workshop you ask about, and your message.

For children: the child's name and age, allergies or needs, the adult collecting, and the parent or guardian's consent and photo choice.

For baking sessions: allergies and dietary needs, and your confirmation of the kitchen notice.

The page you arrived on and campaign details in that address (such as utm fields, gclid or fbclid), so we can see which visit led to a booking.

Enrolment and billing details for students in weekly classes, and messages you send us on WhatsApp or email.

Why we collect it

To confirm bookings, arrange trial classes and run classes and workshops safely, including knowing about allergies.

To manage enrolment, billing and attendance for weekly classes, and to tell you about your classes.

To publish photographs of sessions and work, only with the photo choice made when booking.

To measure our advertising, using Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta pixel through Google Tag Manager. These record which pages are viewed and which buttons are pressed, not the contents of your messages.

Photographs of children

We never publish a photograph or video that identifies a child without consent from a parent or guardian, and we never publish a child's name. You can withdraw consent at any time by messaging us, and the photo will be taken down.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. It is shared only with the service providers that run our website, data storage (Supabase), messaging (WhatsApp) and advertising measurement (Google and Meta), and only as far as needed to run the studio.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as reasonably useful for following up, and student records for the time a student is with us plus a reasonable period afterwards for accounting. Consent records are kept for as long as the related photo is in use.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent (including photo consent) at any time. Contact us using the details below.
