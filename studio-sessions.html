Before you book

The rules the line runs on

Payment

  • Prepaid, and non-refundable
  • Free reschedule within 24 hours of booking
  • RM10 a person after that
  • 10% extra on a public holiday

The room

  • A session runs with at least 3 people
  • Under 3 confirmed 48 hours before the start, we reschedule it or refund you
  • Arrive 15 minutes early
  • Saturday is closed. No workshop Friday after 12:30
