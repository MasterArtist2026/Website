Our teachers

Every class is led by someone who still practises the craft they teach.

Sabrina Hew

Founder · Voice

Founder of Master Artist and an international recording artist, with recordings and concert performances across the United States. These days her passion is helping children and adults find their own voice, musically and in art, and that is a large part of why Master Artist exists.

Cheong Chui Yen

Art lead

Trained in fine arts, with years of experience teaching drawing and painting, from young children through to adults. Technique first, always taught with patience.

Beverly Thien

Beverly Thien

Ana Castro

Baking instructor · vegan baker from Colombia

Alden

Photographer

Founder of WaffleBox Productions, with years of experience shooting graduations, events, food and products. Alden has an eye for turning everyday moments into images worth keeping, and loves sharing it with anyone curious enough to pick up a camera.

Zimin

Videographer

Behind the camera and in the edit, Zimin brings a filmmaker's eye to every project, turning raw footage into stories worth watching, and helps students find their own visual voice.
