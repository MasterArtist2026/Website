Terms & Enrolment

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Trial classes

A trial class costs RM50. If you enrol afterward, that RM50 is credited in full toward your first month's fees. There's no obligation to continue after the trial.

Enrolling and fees

Current programme fees are listed on our Pricing page. Monthly fees cover four weekly sessions, and session packs are valid for 2 months. Registration is RM100 per family (free for the Founding 50). We do not take a deposit, and all materials are included. Classes run subject to minimum enrolment.

Class sizes

We keep classes small, so every student gets real, individual attention from their teacher.

Cancellations and make-up classes

If you need to reschedule or cancel a class, please message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will help you find another time.

Photo and media consent

We may photograph or film classes and student work for use on this website and our social media. We only publish images that identify a specific child where a parent or guardian has given written consent, recorded against that image. You can withdraw consent at any time — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Conduct and safety

Teaching staff are vetted before working with children, and parents are welcome to observe classes at any time. We ask that students and accompanying family members treat staff, other students and the studio space with respect.
