Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll get back to you during opening hours.

How much does a trial class cost?

RM50. If you enrol afterward, that RM50 is credited in full to your first month.

What ages do you teach?

From age 4 through to adults. Each programme lists its own age range, so you can see the right starting point at a glance.

Do you teach adults as well as children?

Yes — Adult Mornings run on Tuesdays and Fridays, 10:30am–12:30pm, with Adult Art and one-to-one Adult Voice & Piano. Photography and Videography & Film are open to adults too.

Does my child need any experience or their own equipment to start?

No — a trial class is designed for complete beginners, and everything you need for that first class is provided. If you continue with an instrument-based class like piano, we'll let you know what's useful to have at home once you've enrolled.

How do I know which programme is right for my child?

Message us on WhatsApp with your child's age and interests and we'll suggest a good starting point — or just book a trial in whichever programme catches their eye first.

Can I stay and watch a class?

Yes — parents are welcome to sit in on any class, any time. Nothing happens behind a closed door.

What are your opening hours?

Sunday to Thursday, 10:30am–7pm. Friday, 10:30am–4pm. Closed Saturdays.

Can musicians from other schools join the Mont Kiara Orchestra?

Yes — the orchestra is open to musicians of any school or teacher, and all instruments are welcome. It practises every Friday at 3pm and performs 2–4 times a month.

Do I need my own camera for Photography?

If you have a camera, bring it — any brand works, because concepts are taught on the whiteboard rather than one camera's menus. No camera yet? Message us and we'll talk you through the options.

How do I book a trial?

Fill in the short form at the bottom of any page, or message us on WhatsApp, and we'll get you booked in for a time that works.
