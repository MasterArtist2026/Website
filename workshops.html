Workshops

Make something. Meet people.

Baking, painting and hands-on craft sessions at Master Artist — as much about the people in the room as what you leave with.

Our workshops sit alongside the regular programmes as a more relaxed, drop-in way to spend a few hours making something with your hands. They're built around community as much as craft — a chance to meet other creative people from the neighbourhood, whether you come alone, bring a friend, or make it a family outing.

Why We Run Them

More than a class — a community

Workshops are open to all ages, and many of the people who come back regularly say it's less about finishing a project and more about the couple of hours spent making something alongside other people. That's the idea: a growing community of makers, built one session at a time.
