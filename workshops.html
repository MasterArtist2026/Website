Workshops · All Ages

Make something. Meet people.

Baking, painting and hands-on craft sessions at Master Artist — as much about the people in the room as what you leave with.

See upcoming workshopsBakingPaintingCraft

Our workshops sit alongside the regular programmes as a more relaxed, drop-in way to spend a few hours making something with your hands. They're built around community as much as craft — a chance to meet other creative people from the neighbourhood.

Baking

Baking Workshops

Measuring, mixing, decorating — and eating. Hands-on sessions baking real recipes like focaccia and banana bread, for anyone who wants to bake something and take it home.

What you'll do

  • Measure, mix and bake a real recipe
  • Decorate and finish your bake
  • Take home what you made

Who it's for

  • All ages — come alone, with a friend, or as a family
  • No prior experience needed
  • Anyone who wants to make something in good company

Why We Run Them

More than a class — a community

Workshops are open to all ages, and many of the people who come back regularly say it's less about finishing a project and more about the couple of hours spent making something alongside other people.

That's the idea: a growing community of makers, built one session at a time.
