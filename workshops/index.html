Group packages

Good to know

Payment and refunds

Seats are prepaid, non-refundable. We confirm on WhatsApp and send payment details there.

Changing your date

Reschedules are free within 24h of booking, RM10 per person after. Please give us 48 hours' notice.

Minimum numbers

Each session runs with a minimum of 6 people. If one doesn't reach it, we'll message you to move to another date.

Arriving

Please arrive 15 minutes early. We're upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas.

Public holidays

Public holiday sessions are 10% more, except Sunday 8 November.

Launch Season prices

The prices on this page are Launch Season prices, until 30 April 2027.
